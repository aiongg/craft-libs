# craft-imaging

Raster image editing for Craft apps. With the `ui` feature, `craft_imaging::ui` has the `WarpGrid` (a warp's mesh on a canvas, with `WarpMesh`, `WarpEdit` and the press and release helpers) and the `LevelsEditor` (a channel's `Levels` over its histogram), with their stories and words (`craft_imaging::ui::stories()`, `craft_imaging::ui::WORDS`).

The curves editor and the histogram stay in `craft-ui`.
